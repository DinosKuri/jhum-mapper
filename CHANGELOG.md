# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-10)

First public release: final detection rule, standalone tool, annual series 1989–2025,
calibration and validation points for 2025 and 2010, experiment inputs, interpretation protocol.

### Development history

| Version | Change | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | Median composites, adjacent seasons | UA 0.44, PA 0.30 |
| v2 | Asymmetric percentiles, Oct–Jan against Mar–May | Background dNBR rose to 0.145 |
| v3 | Anniversary windows, Mar–May, 75/25 percentiles | Background dNBR fell to 0.004 |
| v6 | Wide anniversary windows, 25/25, plus narrow branch | UA 0.886, PA 0.901 (2025) |
